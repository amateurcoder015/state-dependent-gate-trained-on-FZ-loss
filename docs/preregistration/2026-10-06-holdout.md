# Pre-registration: held-out asset study

Written and committed on 2026-10-06, before any data for the held-out assets was downloaded or inspected. The commit timestamp on GitHub is the record of this order.

## Why

On the development universe (8 NSE assets, test period 2023-01 to 2026-09), the original FZ-trained gate did worse than equal weights because its scale head learned g ≈ 0.82 and under-forecast risk in 2024. Two follow-ups came out of that development work:

1. A revised gate (`gate_v2`) that pulls the scale factor toward 1.
2. An exploratory finding: the original gate does better than equal weights when volatility-of-volatility and the variance risk premium are high.

Both were chosen after looking at development-universe test results. This study tests them once, on assets never used before.

## Changes made before registration (development universe only)

- `gate_v2` = the original gate plus a penalty `lam_g * mean((log g)^2)` with `lam_g = 10`, fixed (not tuned on validation). Everything else is identical to `gate` (features, folds, λ_eq grid {0, 0.1, 1, 10}, 5 seeds, hyperparameters).
- `gate_v2` was run once on the development universe before registration: pooled mean FZ0 at α = 2.5% was -3.2519, against -3.2491 for equal weights and -3.2580 for Taylor minimum score; its mean scale factor was 0.98–0.99 in every fold. No other variant was tried.

## Protocol (identical to the development study unless stated)

- Data 2015-01-01 to 2026-09-30 from Yahoo Finance, frozen with SHA-256 manifest.
- Outlier rule: every |r| > 10% is reviewed; a row is dropped only if raw and adjusted prices show an unadjusted corporate action (split, bonus, demerger) at that date. All other rows are kept.
- Base models, refit schedule, baselines (mean, median, Taylor minimum and relative score, previous best), gate folds, seeds and hyperparameters exactly as in the code at this commit. The gate is trained only on the held-out assets (pooled across them).
- Variants run at α = 2.5%: `gate`, `gate_v2`, `gate_noscale`, `gate_qlike`. `gate_v2` also at α = 1% and 5%.
- Test period 2023-01-01 to 2026-09-30. Pooled tests use the cross-asset average loss per date.

## Hypotheses and decision rules (α = 2.5%, pooled)

- **H1 (primary).** `gate_v2` has lower mean FZ0 loss than the equal-weight mean. Test: Diebold–Mariano with HLN correction; supported if the statistic is negative and one-sided p < 0.05 (two-sided p < 0.10).
- **H1b (secondary).** Same comparison of `gate_v2` against Taylor minimum score and relative score.
- **H2 (replication of the exploratory finding).** For the original `gate`, the HAC regression of (gate − mean) FZ0 on standardized vov30, vix, vrp and loss dispersion gives negative coefficients on vov30 and on vrp, each with one-sided p < 0.05. Also reported for `gate_v2` as exploratory.
- **Reported without a decision rule:** Model Confidence Set (10%), backtests (Kupiec, Christoffersen, DQ, McNeil–Frey), results at α = 1% and 5%, per-asset results.

## Commitments

- The held-out study is run once. No change to code, assets, hyperparameters or tests after the held-out data is downloaded, except fixes for crashes. Any such fix is listed in the results with its reason.
- All hypotheses are reported whatever the outcome.
